# BioAdaptive Interface Lab - Lab Identity

Lab name: BioAdaptive Interface Lab
Institution: Wilfrid Laurier University
Location: 67 Darling St. Market Darling Center Building (MDC107), Brantford, Ontario, Canada
Director: John E. Munoz, PhD
Director role: Assistant Professor of User Experience Design, Liberal Arts, Wilfrid Laurier University, Brantford, Ontario, Canada

Research vision:
The BioAdaptive Interface Lab studies the intersection of human physiology and digital interaction. The lab works on serious games, virtual reality simulations, social robotics, and brain-computer interfaces using human-computer interaction methods. The lab integrates physiological data such as heart rate, muscle activity, and neural signals to build interfaces that adapt to users in real time. Application areas include healthcare, rehabilitation, wellbeing, and training.

Research themes:
- Serious games
- Virtual reality simulations
- Social robotics
- Brain-computer interfaces
- Human-computer interaction
- Physiologically adaptive technologies
- Healthcare and rehabilitation
- Wellbeing and training
