# Research Physiologically Adaptive Games

Description:
This research explores how real-time biosignals such as heart rate, brain activity, and muscle movement can dynamically shape gameplay. By integrating wearable sensors and adaptive algorithms, the lab designs interactive experiences that respond to player emotion, stress, and cognitive state.

Applications:
- Serious games
- Rehabilitation
- Training
- Well-being
