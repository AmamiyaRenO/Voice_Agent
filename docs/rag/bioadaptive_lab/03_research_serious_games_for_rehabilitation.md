# Research Serious Games For Rehabilitation

Description:
This research explores interactive and immersive games that support physical, cognitive, and emotional health. The lab combines adaptive gameplay and exergaming methods to promote motor recovery, stress management, and active aging.

Applications:
- Motor recovery
- Stress management
- Active aging
- Accessible rehabilitation
- Long-term wellbeing through play
