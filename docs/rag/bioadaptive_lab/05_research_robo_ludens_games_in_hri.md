# Research Robo Ludens Games In Hri

Description:
This research studies the role of games in human-robot interaction to support collaboration, learning, and social engagement. The lab designs experiences in which humans and robots cooperate, compete, and communicate in meaningful ways.

Applications:
- Social robotics
- Intergenerational play
- Assistive technologies
- Human-robot collaboration

Collaborators:
- Kerstin Dautenhahn
- Alex Aroyo
- Shruti Chandra

Related concepts:
- Human-robot interaction
- Games in HRI
- Interactive robotics
- Embodied systems
- Social engagement
