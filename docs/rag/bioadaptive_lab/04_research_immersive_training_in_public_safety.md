# Research Immersive Training In Public Safety

Description:
This research uses virtual reality, physiological sensing, and adaptive simulation to improve decision-making, stress management, and skill development for first responders. The lab studies realistic high-pressure training environments for law enforcement, firefighting, and emergency response.

Applications:
- Decision-making
- Stress management
- Skill development
- Situational awareness
- Resilience
- Performance improvement
